2. What this app does

Schätzpoker is a local party quiz game. The app is designed primarily for local gameplay on one device.

3. Data processed in the app

The current app version primarily stores game-related data locally on the device. This includes:

  • played question progress
  • unlocked bonus-question progress for the progressive free pack
  • local player profiles, stats, titles, and mystery unlocks
  • local balances and progress related to Jetons, fragments, research, and question unlocks
  • local unlock status for question packs and purchases
  • app preferences such as sound, default setup, and game settings

This locally stored data is used so the app can remember progress, player identity, unlocks, entitlements, and settings between sessions.

4. Local storage on the device

The app stores parts of its app state locally on the device. According to the current implementation, this local storage is used for:

  • played question IDs
  • unlocked question counts and milestone-based bonus unlock counts
  • entitlement information and locally stored owned billing product IDs
  • Jetons, fragment inventory, fragment-forge history, and research/shop progress
  • player profiles, local stats, title progress, mystery progress, and unlock history
  • app preferences

This data is not uploaded to a Coastforge Studio backend because the app currently does not use its own online backend for accounts, cloud sync, or profile storage.

As a result, deleting app data, uninstalling the app, or switching to another device may remove locally stored progress and locally stored consumable content.

5. Player names and gameplay inputs

Player names and gameplay inputs may be entered during a game session for local use inside the app.

Based on the current app logic, local profile names may also be stored on-device as part of the app's persistent profile and stats system. They are not uploaded to a Coastforge Studio backend in the current app version.

6. In-app purchases and platform services

If in-app purchases are offered, purchase processing is handled through Google Play Billing and related Google platform services.

Google may process purchase-related data as part of providing billing, payment validation, fraud prevention, and purchase restoration. The processing of such platform data is governed by Google's own terms and privacy information.

In the current app structure, non-consumable purchases such as ad-free entitlements may be restored through Google Play where supported. Consumable purchases such as Jetons or fragment packs are currently tied to local app data after delivery and are not currently restored through Google Play once that local app data has been removed.

If consumable currency or fragments are used to unlock local research progress, that resulting local progress may also be lost if the app data is deleted or the app is uninstalled.

7. Advertising and consent

The free version of the app may display ads at designated points in the game flow. According to the current project state, advertising and consent handling may be provided through Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Depending on region and legal requirements, users may be shown a consent flow related to advertising. Google may process device and advertising-related information in accordance with its own services and privacy documentation.

If additional third-party SDKs such as analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync, login, or further advertising services are added in a later release, this privacy policy will be updated before that version is published.

8. Permissions

The Android project currently includes internet access, which may be required for platform services such as Google Play Billing and future release features that rely on online connectivity.

9. Data sharing

According to the current project state:

  • no own backend account system is used
  • no own cloud storage is used
  • no own analytics backend is used
  • advertising and consent handling may involve Google AdMob and Google UMP

If this changes, this policy will be updated before the affected app version is released.

10. Data retention and deletion

Because core app data is currently stored locally on the device, deleting app data or uninstalling the app generally removes locally stored app information from the device, subject to platform behavior.

This may include local player profiles, titles, mystery progress, Jetons, fragments, research progress, and locally unlocked question content.

If purchase entitlements are linked to non-consumable Google Play products, restoration may still be possible through Google Play where applicable. Consumable purchases and locally stored progress connected to those consumables are currently not guaranteed to be recoverable after deletion of app data or uninstallation.
